1: Steps

The Donation Process

Step 01: Intake

Registration & Health Check

  • Temperature < 99.5°F (37.5°C)
  • BP: Systolic 90–180 / Diastolic < 100
  • Hb Level: ≥12.5 g/dL (F) / 13.0 g/dL (M)
Step 02: Extraction

The Donation

Whole blood collection typically yields ~500mL (one pint) in under 10 minutes. Apheresis can take 1–2 hours but isolates specific components like platelets.

Step 03: Analysis

Processing

One unit is separated into Red Cells, Plasma, and Platelets via high-speed centrifugation.

Safety Testing
HIV, Hep B/C, West Nile
Blood Typing
ABO & Rh Factor Confirmation
Step 04: Storage

Preservation & Use

  • RBC: 42 Days @ 1–6°C
  • Platelets: 5 Days @ Room Temp
  • Plasma: 1 Year (Frozen)

03: Screening

Donor Requirements

Age & Weight

Minimum 17 years (16 with consent) and ≥110 lbs (50 kg) for safety.

Waiting Period

Whole blood requires a 56-day reset. Platelets can be donated every 7 days.

General Health

Donors must be symptom-free of colds or flu on the day of donation.

4: Facts

Myth vs Reality

"Donating weakens the immune system."

While you lose some white cells, your body regenerates them within hours. There is no long-term immune suppression.

MEDICAL FACT: FALSE

"I'm too old to donate."

There is no upper age limit for blood donation in many regions, provided the donor is in good health.

MEDICAL FACT: FALSE

"Tattoos disqualify me permanently."

In most regions, a 3-month deferral applies if the tattoo was done in an unregulated shop; otherwise, no wait is required.

MEDICAL FACT: FALSE

"The needle is extremely painful."

The sensation is comparable to a brief pinch. Most donors report that anxiety is more noticeable than the physical event.
